Privacy policy

Fitness challenge app · fitness.amogh.systems · Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: we collect only what the challenges need, you choose who sees your optional profile and health details, health information is private unless you share it, we never sell your data or show ads, and backups are encrypted before they leave our server.

Who runs the app

The app is run by the organisers of the fitness challenges held in it. Accounts are created by a challenge administrator, who gives you a temporary password that you change at your first sign-in.

What we collect

  • Account: username, display name and your password (stored only as a one-way hash, never readable).
  • Optional profile: full name, mobile number, email, date of birth, gender, location, a short "about" text and a profile photo. You choose for each item who may see it.
  • Challenge activity: the step screenshots you submit, the step count and date read from them, rest days, exemption requests and their evidence, votes, bonus activities, points and results.
  • Payments: the amount, date and transaction reference (UTR) of contributions you report, and any payment proof you upload. Payments are made outside the app; we never see or store card or bank details.
  • Community: posts, photos, comments, reactions and reports you make in a challenge's community feed.
  • Optional health profile (only after you give explicit consent): height, weight, BMI, body fat, waist, resting heart rate, fitness and activity level, goals and any health considerations you add.
  • Security and activity records: sign-ins and sign-outs, actions taken in the app, and the IP address and device/browser they came from; technical error reports.
  • Phone notifications: if you switch them on, the delivery address your phone's browser provides.

How we use it

  • To run the challenges: check step evidence, apply the rules, keep score, manage contributions, fines, refunds and prizes.
  • To show you your progress, and to show others only what you have chosen to share.
  • To send reminders and alerts you asked for.
  • To keep the app secure, investigate problems and keep an honest record of who changed what.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use third-party analytics or trackers.

Who can see your information

  • Other participants see your display name, points and the leaderboard of challenges you take part in, your community posts, and any profile or health items you have chosen to share with them.
  • Challenge admins see what they need to run the challenge (your submissions, rest days, exemptions, payments and contact number for reminders). They see health information only if you share it with them.
  • The finance manager sees payment records. Their role gives no access to health information.
  • Super Admins run the app and see the activity log. Their role gives no access to your health information; you can grant one of them temporary support access, which ends by itself and is recorded.

Health information

The health profile is optional and is only collected after you give explicit consent. It is private by default; you choose, item by item, whether your challenge admins, challenge members or everyone in the app may see it. This is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app's screens. Health information never appears in leaderboards, notifications, community posts, admin screens or logs. The numbers are for tracking only and are not medical advice. You can change or delete any entry, withdraw consent (which stops all sharing immediately) or delete all your health information at any time.

Outside services we use

  • Step screenshot reading is currently done on our own server; screenshots are not sent to outside services.
  • Phone notifications are delivered by your phone's notification service (Apple or Google). They carry only a short title, never your details.
  • WhatsApp reminders may be sent to you by a challenge admin from their own WhatsApp, using the mobile number on your account.
  • Backups are encrypted on our server and then stored in the organisers' Google Drive. Google cannot read them. The app uses Google's Drive service only to store and manage these encrypted backup files, and can access only the files it created itself.

How long we keep it

  • Challenge records (submissions, points, payments, results) are kept so results and money stay accountable.
  • Health information is kept until you delete it.
  • Technical error reports are deleted after 180 days. The activity log is kept as a permanent record.
  • Backups are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically.

Security

The app is only available over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, sessions are protected, every action is checked against your role, uploaded files are only shown to people allowed to see them, and backups are encrypted before they are copied off the server.

Your choices and rights

  • View and change your profile, its visibility and your photo at any time (Profile).
  • Turn phone notifications on or off (Profile).
  • Manage, share, withdraw or delete your health information (Profile → Health profile).
  • Delete your own community posts and comments.
  • For anything else - a copy of your data, a correction, or closing your account - ask a challenge admin.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and important changes will be announced in the app.
